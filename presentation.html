The Problem

3D modeling is powerful — but locked behind training

  • Modeling an object takes hours of software training or paid expertise — and it is tedious.
  • Complex CAD tools assume a skill level most beginners do not have.
  • The people who need 3D models most — students, teachers, makers, small workshops — are least likely to have the training.
  • Describing an object in words is unreliable and prone to errors.

How It Works

Two paths, one pipeline

🎙️ Voice Path

  1. Hold Q and speak → speech to text.
  2. Ollama routes intent: GENERATE_3D, CAMERA, or CHAT.
  3. Known objects resolve instantly from a local catalog; unknown ones go to Gemini.

📷 Camera Path

  1. Capture the sharpest recent frame.
  2. Gemini Vision returns a part-by-part primitive model.
  3. Geometry audit snaps floaters, drops specks, validates the layout.
Both paths end in the same viewer — with hand tracking and STL/OBJ export.
